    Why This Model is Efficient for DR Detection
    NoisyStudent Pretraining
    Pretrained on 300M unlabeled images via self-training — providing far richer feature representations than standard ImageNet weights, critical for scarce medical data.
    CLAHE Preprocessing
    Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization enhances subtle lesions — microaneurysms, hemorrhages, exudates — that are invisible in raw fundus images.
    High-Res 380×380 Input
    Larger input resolution than standard EfficientNet-B0 (224px) preserves microaneurysms (~10–125 µm) that are lost at lower resolutions.
    Clinically-Aligned QWK
    Optimized for Quadratic Weighted Kappa — the ophthalmology gold standard — which penalizes by grade distance rather than treating all misclassifications equally.
    0.991 No-DR AUC
    Near-perfect sensitivity for ruling out DR in healthy patients — the most critical clinical use-case — acting as a reliable first-pass screening filter.
    Efficient Deployment
    ~20M parameters with compound scaling achieves higher accuracy than ResNet-50 (25M) and far surpasses VGG-16 (138M) — smaller, faster, better.

    Dataset & Disclaimer
    APTOS 2019 Dataset
    • ~3,662 retinal fundus images
    • Graded by expert ophthalmologists (0–4 scale)
    • High class imbalance (majority No-DR)
    • Variable image quality, lighting & cameras
    • Addressed with weighted sampling & augmentation
